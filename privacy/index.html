Privacy Policy

Crown Grid. Effective date: 2026-10-05

What the app stores

Your progress, times, statistics and settings are saved only on this device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Accounts and personal data

Crown Grid has no sign-up or login and does not ask for personal data such as name, email, phone number or location.

Ads

Hints and extra lives come from rewarded ads, and short ads may appear after some wins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use the advertising ID and other device identifiers under Google's privacy policy. Where required, we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and you can change it in the app's Privacy screen.

Analytics and crashes

Anonymous usage events (such as difficulty, board size, time, hints and ad type) and crash reports are sent to Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve the game. Firebase Analytics uses an app instance ID. These data contain no personal information.

Purchases

The one-time "remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing and verified by RevenueCat using an anonymous identifier. We never see your payment details.

Children

Crown Grid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

There is no account and we hold no personal data. Game data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
